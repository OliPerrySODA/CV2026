## Leadership and operating style
- Training is the best investment. Great recruitment firms act like world class learning and development operations.
- Measure what matters. Inputs help most people, outcomes matter most.
- Clear communication reduces noise. Difficult conversations should happen early.
- Automation lifts net fee income per head when data is organised and workflows are tight.
- Community and face to face time win. Roundtables, coffee, and dinners outperform most outbound channels.
- Personal brand is a differentiator in a low trust market.
